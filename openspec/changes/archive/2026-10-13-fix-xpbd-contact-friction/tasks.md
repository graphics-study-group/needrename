## 9. Commit 3 — the tangential accumulators become vectors, and the kinetic cap becomes a projection

The cone and the cap bound a contact's accumulated tangential impulse, whose direction is the drift or sliding direction `t`; `t` is recomputed every iteration and rotates when other constraints move the material points, so the original scalar accumulators enforced the strictly stronger `sum |increment| <= bound`. That is safe (the triangle inequality means the vector sum can never leave the cone) but not tight: self-cancelling corrections consume the budget, and once it is spent the gate abstains for the rest of the substep. Reported by the user as a correctness objection to the position phase's `tangent_lagrange` accumulation, confirmed, and extended to the velocity phase's `p_friction`. Design: D14.

The same objection applies one level down in the velocity phase, and the user raised it separately: making the accumulator a vector is not enough while the *increment* is still bounded by a scalar remainder, because the remainder form cannot unwind and cannot rotate the total. The velocity bound therefore became a projection of the accumulated total (D15), which is the formulation every surveyed solver uses.
